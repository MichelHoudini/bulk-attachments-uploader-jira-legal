Bulk Attachments Uploader for Jira

Data Processing Addendum

Effective date: 16 September 2026

This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) forms part of the End-User Terms between Michel Di Lorenzo / Algomind (“Processor”, “we”, “us”) and the customer that installs or uses Bulk Attachments Uploader for Jira (“Controller”, “you”). It applies only where, and to the extent, applicable data-protection law treats the customer as a controller and Algomind as a processor of personal data.

1. Processing instructions and purpose

Processor will process personal data only on Controller’s documented instructions, including the actions initiated by authorized Jira users through the app, and only to provide the app’s file-to-Jira issue mapping, validation, and attachment-upload functionality. Processor will not sell, share for advertising, profile, or independently reuse customer content.

2. Processing details

The processing may concern Jira issue keys and user-selected attachment files, including filenames and file content, where those contain personal data. Data subjects may include Controller’s authorized Jira users and individuals referenced in Jira issues or selected files. Processing is limited to the duration of the user’s active browser session and the customer’s use of the app.

3. Hosting and retention

The app runs on Atlassian Forge and uses Atlassian’s requestJira bridge. Algomind does not operate an external backend, database, file-storage service, analytics service, or vendor-controlled customer-data store for the app. Selected files and mappings are held only in the user’s browser session. Uploaded files become attachments in the customer’s Jira Cloud site. Algomind does not retain an independent copy after the session.

4. Confidentiality and security

Processor will ensure that persons authorized to process personal data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations and will use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the limited processing described here. The app uses the Jira permissions of the authenticated user and Atlassian Forge’s platform controls.

5. Assistance and incidents

Taking account of the nature of the processing, Processor will reasonably assist Controller with applicable data-subject requests and security inquiries relating to the app. Because Algomind does not retain a separate copy of customer content, requests concerning Jira-stored attachments should ordinarily be handled through the customer’s Jira Cloud site. Processor will notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed security incident involving Processor’s app-related processing, where notification is required by applicable law.

6. Subprocessors and transfers

The app relies on Atlassian Forge and the customer’s Jira Cloud site to provide the app functionality. Atlassian’s processing of Forge data is governed by Atlassian’s Forge terms and data-processing documentation. Algomind does not engage a separate vendor-controlled hosting, storage, analytics, or remote-processing subprocessor for this app. Algomind does not independently transfer customer content to a third-party service.

7. Deletion and return

At the end of a browser session or use of the app, Algomind has no independent persistent copy of the selected files, mappings, or Jira content to return or delete. Content already uploaded to Jira remains subject to Controller’s Jira configuration and Atlassian’s handling of Jira data.

8. Controller responsibilities

Controller is responsible for ensuring it has an appropriate legal basis and authority for the Jira data and files its users choose to process with the app, and for configuring its Jira permissions and retention settings.

9. Contact

Questions, data-subject requests, and security inquiries concerning this DPA can be sent to algomind.atelier@gmail.com.
